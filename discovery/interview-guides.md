# Interview guides

The interviewer uses open questions only. No leading questions, no solution pitching, no mentioning AI agents or any product. Each interview opens with a role and context check and closes with "what did I not ask that I should have?".

## Restaurant manager (Yolanda Ferrer, Benidorm Playa)

1. Walk me through last Saturday, from open to close. Where did hiring tasks appear?
2. Think of the last person you hired. How did the whole thing start, and how long until their first shift?
3. Where do you keep names of people you might hire someday?
4. What has to be true before you put someone new on the rota?
5. When does hiring work get in the way of service? Tell me about the last time.
6. What would make you stop using a tool you were given?

## Crew trainer (Iker Lozano, Benidorm Playa)

1. Walk me through a new hire's first three days from your side.
2. When is a new hire actually useful to you? When are they a burden?
3. Tell me about the last person who quit in week one. What happened?
4. What do new hires arrive knowing and not knowing?
5. Who do you blame when a new hire arrives unprepared?

## Head of Talent Acquisition (Jordi Puig, HQ)

1. Walk me through what happens after an application lands in HireDesk.
2. Who screens, when, and by what criteria? Show me the last one you reviewed.
3. What does your team's weekly rhythm look like?
4. Where does your funnel leak the most, and how do you know?
5. What data in HireDesk do you trust and not trust?
6. What would losing control over hiring decisions mean for you?

## Area Operations Director (Raul Sempere, Costa Blanca)

1. Walk me through how you knew which restaurants would break last July.
2. What do you do when a restaurant tells you on Friday that they need someone for Saturday?
3. How much do you trust the coverage numbers you see?
4. Tell me about a time understaffing cost you sales or service time.
5. What is your relationship with the HR team on hiring speed?

## IT Integration Lead (Hugo Andrade)

1. Walk me through the systems a new hire touches, from application to first payroll.
2. Which integrations work well today, and which are fragile?
3. What are the hard limits of each system (rate limits, webhooks, exports)?
4. What would you need to see before allowing a vendor to write into these systems?
5. Tell me about the last integration incident and what it taught you.

## DPO (Laura Cebrian)

1. Walk me through where candidate data lives today and who can see it.
2. What happens to rejected candidates' data? And to WhatsApp messages managers keep?
3. What would a compliant AI-assisted process need to show you?
4. Where is the biggest compliance risk in the current process?
5. What would make you say no to a new tool in this space?

## People Ops and Payroll lead (Sofia Marques)

1. Walk me through what you need before you can register a new employee.
2. Which documents arrive late most often, and what does that cost you?
3. Tell me about the last person who could not start on time because of paperwork.
4. How do you chase missing documents today?
5. What does "ready to start" mean for you, exactly?

## Chief People Officer (Marta Ibanez)

1. What did the board ask you, in their words?
2. Walk me through the last summer: where did hiring hurt the most?
3. What would make this a win for you by June?
4. What are you afraid of in this project?
5. Who has to say yes before anything goes live?

## Candidates (A, B, C) and churned new hire

1. Walk me through the last jobs you applied for: where, how, what happened.
2. How did the chain that hired you first stand out from the others?
3. What did nobody ask you that they should have?
4. (Churned) Walk me through your first week. Where did it break?
5. What would make you answer a message from an unknown number?
