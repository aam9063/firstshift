# Interview transcript: Iker Lozano

- **Role:** Crew Trainer
- **Restaurant:** Lumbre Burger Benidorm Playa (drive-thru, 38 crew, coastal seasonal unit)
- **Simulated date:** Friday, 16 October 2026, 15:20, crew room before the evening shift
- **Duration:** approximately 30 minutes
- **Context:** Discovery interview conducted in English with Spanish quotes verbatim. The interviewer follows the crew trainer guide in `discovery/interview-guides.md` and asks open questions only.

---

[T-iker-01] **Interviewer:** Thanks for sitting down with me before your shift. Quick check first: you are the crew trainer here at Benidorm Playa, which means you are the one who receives and trains new hires, is that right?

[T-iker-02] **Iker:** Sí, nueve años en Lumbre, y desde hace cuatro soy el trainer. Every new person this restaurant hires, they spend their first shifts next to me.

[T-iker-03] **Interviewer:** Walk me through a new hire's first three days, from your side.

[T-iker-04] **Iker:** Day one is uniform, tour, and the till if they are lucky. Day two I start the stations, one at a time, grill last because it is the one that needs the hygiene module done. Day three depends. "Todo depende de cómo lleguen," and that is not up to me.

[T-iker-05] **Interviewer:** What decides how they arrive, in your experience?

[T-iker-06] **Iker:** One thing, mostly: the food hygiene module in LearnBite. "Si no está hecho, no pueden tocar comida. Ni un pan." If it is not done, they cannot touch food, so day one is the till or the cleaning, and the training I planned does not happen.

[T-iker-07] **Interviewer:** How often does that happen, that the module is not done?

[T-iker-08] **Iker:** Often enough that I plan for it. Of the last five that landed with me, two arrived without the module. "Y no es que lleguen tarde, es que llegan sin empezar." Nobody tells me in advance either, I find out that morning.

[T-iker-09] **Interviewer:** What do you do with them on those days?

[T-iker-10] **Iker:** Till, lobby, "turno de trapos," as we say. Cleaning. It is honest work but it is not what they came for, and I have a full station to run while I invent tasks for someone I cannot put on the grill. That day the trainer is the burden, not them.

[T-iker-11] **Interviewer:** When is a new hire actually useful to you? And when are they a burden?

[T-iker-12] **Iker:** Useful from shift three or four, if the module was done and they show up. Before that they cost more than they give, that is normal, I accept it. A burden is when week two arrives and they still cannot touch food, because then nothing I taught them sticks and we start again.

[T-iker-13] **Interviewer:** Tell me about the last person who quit in week one. What happened?

[T-iker-14] **Iker:** Not one. Two of my last five left in the first week. "Y los dos dijeron lo mismo: esto no es lo que me dijeron." Same words, both of them, one on day four and one on day six.

[T-iker-15] **Interviewer:** What were they told, do you think, and what did they find?

[T-iker-16] **Iker:** They were promised hours, a start, a proper job. What they found: first rota late and different from what they expected, two or three days of cleaning because of the module, and nobody explaining anything until they asked me. "Eso no lo dije yo, pero tampoco lo desmentí." Nobody owns the promise.

[T-iker-17] **Interviewer:** What do new hires arrive knowing, and not knowing?

[T-iker-18] **Iker:** Some arrive knowing how to treat a customer, that you cannot teach in a week, and that is gold here with the tourists. What they do not know is the basics of arriving: the hygiene module, the uniform, what shift they actually have, what the work is. "Saben atender, no saben empezar."

[T-iker-19] **Interviewer:** Who do you blame when a new hire arrives unprepared?

[T-iker-20] **Iker:** I do not like the word blame. Between the office that hires them and me there is a silence, and I live on this side of it. "A mí nadie me llama. Nadie me dice: Iker, el lunes entra uno, tal y cual." The paper arrives, the person arrives, and the information arrives never.

[T-iker-21] **Interviewer:** For a first day to go well, what would need to be true when they walk in?

[T-iker-22] **Iker:** Three things. Module done before they walk in, rota known by them and by me, and them knowing what the job actually is, "que sepan a lo que vienen." With those three, I can make anyone useful in a week. Without them, I cannot even start.

[T-iker-23] **Interviewer:** We are nearly at time. What did I not ask that I should have?

[T-iker-24] **Iker:** You are asking me, and I stay. "Pregunten a los que se fueron, no a mí." The two who left in week one, they know why this breaks. I can only tell you what it looks like from the side that stayed.
