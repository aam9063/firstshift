- **What surprised me:** the deciding factor was not the job itself but response speed: applied at 18:00, WhatsApp reply before 19:00, hired over six other chains that stayed silent [T-candidate-a-08 to T-candidate-a-12].
- **What contradicts HQ's description:** the funnel is not a queue that processes patiently; a 19-year-old applies to six chains in one evening and takes the first that answers. Also her first-shift time reached her by chance, via a crew WhatsApp group, the night before [T-candidate-a-16].
- **What I still do not know:** how she would react to an automated first reply if it felt templated, and what share of her cohort never gets any answer at all (her other five applications all went silent).
