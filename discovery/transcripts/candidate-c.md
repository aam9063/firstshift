# Interview transcript: Candidate C

- **Role profile:** Portuguese national, 22, seasonal worker in Benidorm
- **Simulated date:** 2027-01-23, 12:30
- **Channel:** WhatsApp voice call (candidate has worked two summers in Benidorm hospitality and is looking for a contract beyond the season)
- **Context:** recruited for Lumbre Burger Group discovery interviews; candidate speaks Portuguese, Spanish and English and has never been hired in a drive-thru despite tourist-area demand.

---

[T-candidate-c-01] **Interviewer:** Hi, thanks for answering. I'm doing research with people who have worked hospitality jobs in Benidorm, to understand how the hiring felt from your side. No form, no test, just a conversation.

[T-candidate-c-02] **Candidate:** Oi! No problem, I have the break now, I talk with you.

[T-candidate-c-03] **Interviewer:** Tell me about your last two summers. How did you find those jobs?

[T-candidate-c-04] **Candidate:** The first year, 2025, I came from Portugal with my cousin, she worked here already. I walked the street with printed CVs, like 30 copies. Now, last year, I did the forms with the codes on the windows, easier, but the same, you send and then nothing for many days.

[T-candidate-c-05] **Interviewer:** What languages do you work in during a normal shift?

[T-candidate-c-06] **Candidate:** Everything. Portuguese with some workers and some tourists, Spanish with the team and the Spanish clients, English with the tourists, and a little of everything, French, German words. In the beach bars it is normal, one client does not speak Spanish and I am the one who can take the order.

[T-candidate-c-07] **Interviewer:** Did any of the places you applied to ever ask you about your languages?

[T-candidate-c-08] **Candidate:** No, never. Only "do you have experience" and "availability". I put in the CV I speak three languages, but nobody asked one question about it. I find it strange, in the centre and in the drive-thru, half the cars are foreigners, no?

[T-candidate-c-09] **Interviewer:** That surprises me. Let me understand better: did you ever apply for a drive-thru unit?

[T-candidate-c-10] **Candidate:** Yes, two times, one Lumbre and one other. I did the form with the QR. The Lumbre answered me by WhatsApp after some days, which is already more than the other. But the questions were normal, experience, hours, if I have the papers. Nothing about languages, not one word.

[T-candidate-c-11] **Interviewer:** What happened after that WhatsApp exchange?

[T-candidate-c-12] **Candidate:** They said the manager would call me. I waited, maybe one week. Then in March last year I already had the beach bar contract for the summer, so I stopped waiting. I cannot stay with the phone waiting, the rent in Benidorm does not wait.

[T-candidate-c-13] **Interviewer:** If the manager had called, what would you have wanted them to ask?

[T-candidate-c-14] **Candidate:** The languages, first. And maybe to meet fast. In Benidorm everything is fast, one week and the client is here. For me a job is the same, if they want me for the summer, ask me in February, not in May.

[T-candidate-c-15] **Interviewer:** Tell me about your contracts so far. How long did they run?

[T-candidate-c-16] **Candidate:** Always seasonal, May to October, more or less. It is okay but every October is the same, goodbye salary, look for work again. This year I want something that continues, even part time in the winter, I stay in Benidorm now.

[T-candidate-c-17] **Interviewer:** And has any restaurant offered you a winter contract?

[T-candidate-c-18] **Candidate:** One bar offered, but cash, no contract, so no. The restaurants, no. I applied in November to some, through the forms again. I think they look at the CV and see "seasonal" and they think I go back to Portugal. But I am here, with NIE, my Spanish is good, my English is good.

[T-candidate-c-19] **Interviewer:** What did you write in those forms when they asked about availability?

[T-candidate-c-20] **Candidate:** "Completa", full, I put yes to everything. For me the problem was never the hours, I did doubles in August. The problem is nobody calls me.

[T-candidate-c-21] **Interviewer:** If a message came from an unknown number about a job, what would make you trust it and answer?

[T-candidate-c-22] **Candidate:** If it says the name of the restaurant, and it speaks like a person, and it asks something real, like "when can you start" or "can you come tomorrow". If it is only a link, no. And if they write in English to me first, actually I like it, it shows they saw my profile.

[T-candidate-c-23] **Interviewer:** Last question: what did I not ask that I should have?

[T-candidate-c-24] **Candidate:** Why the restaurants in a place like Benidorm do not search for the languages. It is the most important thing here, more than the experience, the experience I learn in two days. I know people from Brazil, from Romania, they speak three, four languages, and they send forms and nothing. The restaurants lose sales and we lose work. Isso não faz sentido, no?

[T-candidate-c-25] **Interviewer:** No, it does not. Thank you very much, this was very helpful.

[T-candidate-c-26] **Candidate:** Obrigada a você! Good luck!

## Interviewer notes

- **What surprised me:** nobody in two years of applications ever asked about her three languages, in a tourist drive-thru town where half the clients are foreign [T-candidate-c-08, T-candidate-c-10, T-candidate-c-24].
- **What contradicts HQ's description:** availability is recorded as a vague "completa" instead of matched to real gaps, and "seasonal" on a CV reads as will-leave rather than available-now, so committed local residents get silently dropped [T-candidate-c-18, T-candidate-c-20].
- **What I still do not know:** whether language skill can be verified cheaply in a chat conversation without becoming a scored "test", and how many winter-capable candidates like her are already sitting in old applicant pools.
