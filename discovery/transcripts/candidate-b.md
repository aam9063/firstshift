# Interview transcript: Candidate B

- **Role profile:** Moroccan national, 34, resident in Spain with NIE, second job
- **Simulated date:** 2027-01-21, 21:10
- **Channel:** WhatsApp voice call (candidate applied twice before to other chains and was rejected for "availability")
- **Context:** recruited for Lumbre Burger Group discovery interviews; candidate currently works mornings in a warehouse and applied to a Lumbre Burger drive-thru unit in Alicante province.

---

[T-candidate-b-01] **Interviewer:** Good evening, thank you for making time after your shift. I'm doing research with people who have applied for crew jobs around Alicante, to understand how the process feels from your side. No test, just conversation.

[T-candidate-b-02] **Candidate:** Buenas, no problem. Now I am free, at home.

[T-candidate-b-03] **Interviewer:** Can you walk me through the last jobs you applied for? Where, how, and what happened?

[T-candidate-b-04] **Candidate:** This one, Lumbre, I applied in December with the code from the window. Before that, two other chains, I sent the same form, maybe one year ago. With the two others, they answered me one email, "unfortunately your profile does not match the current availability".

[T-candidate-b-05] **Interviewer:** What did you understand from that answer?

[T-candidate-b-06] **Candidate:** To be honest? Nothing. I never spoke with any person. Nobody called me, nobody asked me one question. Just the automatic email. In French we say "c'est comme jeter une bouteille dans la mer", you know, like a bottle in the sea.

[T-candidate-b-07] **Interviewer:** When you say availability, what is your actual situation?

[T-candidate-b-08] **Candidate:** I work in a warehouse, mornings. It is fixed, I finish at two. So for a second job I can do every evening from 6, and all weekend, Friday, Saturday, Sunday, day or night. Really, the hours when restaurants need people the most, no?

[T-candidate-b-09] **Interviewer:** Did anyone from those two chains ever ask you which hours you could actually work?

[T-candidate-b-10] **Candidate:** Never. Not one call. I think maybe they see my age, 34, or they see I already have one job and they think no. But they did not ask. Wallah, if someone had called me and said "which shifts can you do", I would have said evenings and weekends, done, it fits perfectly.

[T-candidate-b-11] **Interviewer:** How did the process with Lumbre go instead?

[T-candidate-b-12] **Candidate:** I scanned the code, one form, same as the others. But then a message on WhatsApp the next day, asking when I can talk and which hours I am looking for. I answered: after 18h and weekends. And the person said, "perfect, we need closes on weekends". So we meet.

[T-candidate-b-13] **Interviewer:** What happened at the restaurant itself?

[T-candidate-b-14] **Candidate:** I visited, I spoke with the manager, a normal conversation. She asked about the papers, I have NIE, I have been here seven years, no problem, everything in order. She asked if 25 hours a week on weekends and evenings works with my other job. I said yes, that is exactly why I am here.

[T-candidate-b-15] **Interviewer:** You mentioned your NIE. How was that treated in the process?

[T-candidate-b-16] **Candidate:** With the other chains I never got far enough to show anything. Here they asked for the NIE and bank account by email, one form. I sent photos, done. I know some people, they arrive and they ask for many papers many times, this was only one time, I appreciated that.

[T-candidate-b-17] **Interviewer:** What does a quick, clear answer mean for someone in your situation, looking for a second job?

[T-candidate-b-18] **Candidate:** Everything, because I cannot lose my mornings job to chase a maybe. If I send papers on Monday, I need to know by Friday is it yes or no, and which hours. A quick no is better than a slow maybe. With the other two, a year later, still maybe.

[T-candidate-b-19] **Interviewer:** Did anything in the process feel confusing or uncomfortable?

[T-candidate-b-20] **Candidate:** Only small things. The first form, the "availability" question, again a dropdown, I did not understand it well, I put something. And the salary they told me at the end, when we met, not before. I asked directly, alhamdulillah it was fine, but better to say it in the message, it saves time for everyone.

[T-candidate-b-21] **Interviewer:** If a message arrives from an unknown number about a job, what makes you answer it?

[T-candidate-b-22] **Candidate:** If it says the name of the restaurant and why they write to me, and it asks something concrete, like "can you do Saturday close". Then I answer, even in the break at the warehouse. If it is only "hello, we are a company", no, that is spam.

[T-candidate-b-23] **Interviewer:** Last question: what did I not ask that I should have?

[T-candidate-b-24] **Candidate:** You should ask the companies why they think a man with two jobs is not available. Because I am the most available person for the hours they suffer, the evenings, the weekend. They lose people like me without one phone call. That is my honest answer.

[T-candidate-b-25] **Interviewer:** That is a very clear answer. Thank you, and good luck with both jobs.

[T-candidate-b-26] **Candidate:** Thank you. Bonne journée. Buenas noches.

## Interviewer notes

- **What contradicts HQ's description:** the ATS availability field is a dropdown nobody acts on; the Lumbre process worked when a human asked "which hours" on WhatsApp the next day [T-candidate-b-12]. Salary transparency early would also save everyone time [T-candidate-b-20].
- **What I still do not know:** whether he would accept an AI first contact if it asks a concrete question fast (he answered "what makes you answer an unknown number" with concrete ask plus restaurant name [T-candidate-b-22]), and what document pack he would pre-share with consent.
