# Interview transcript: Yolanda Ferrer

- **Role:** Restaurant Manager
- **Restaurant:** Lumbre Burger Benidorm Playa (drive-thru, 38 crew, coastal seasonal unit)
- **Simulated date:** Friday, 16 October 2026, 10:40, back office after the breakfast rush
- **Duration:** approximately 35 minutes
- **Context:** Discovery interview conducted in English with Spanish quotes verbatim. The interviewer follows the restaurant manager guide in `discovery/interview-guides.md` and asks open questions only.

---

[T-yolanda-01] **Interviewer:** Thanks for making time between shifts. To check we are set up right: you are the restaurant manager here at Benidorm Playa, and your role covers everything from service to rotas, correct?

[T-yolanda-02] **Yolanda:** Sí, eso soy. Everything that happens between these four walls and the drive-thru lane is mine, including the rota and, when we are short, the hiring.

[T-yolanda-03] **Interviewer:** Let's start with last Saturday. Walk me through the day, from open to close. Where did your attention go?

[T-yolanda-04] **Yolanda:** Saturday is drive-thru day. We opened at seven and the lane never stopped until two in the morning. I was on the floor most of it, because we were two short on the close and one of the grill crew called in sick at nine.

[T-yolanda-05] **Interviewer:** During that day, where did hiring tasks appear, if at all?

[T-yolanda-06] **Yolanda:** In the gaps, always in the gaps. Someone texted me about hours while I was on the window, another one in the afternoon. My phone does not stop. "Yolanda, ¿para el verano hay horas?" All day, from people I know.

[T-yolanda-07] **Interviewer:** Think of the last person you hired. How did the whole thing start?

[T-yolanda-08] **Yolanda:** The last one, Laura. She had come through the drive-thru window in August asking if we were taking people on, and I told her to send me a message. In September I had a gap on closes, so I wrote to her first. I did not wait for anything.

[T-yolanda-09] **Interviewer:** And how long from that message to her first shift?

[T-yolanda-10] **Yolanda:** She said yes the same day, of course. Then, hasta que subió al rota... eleven days, I think. Eleven days with a person who said yes to my face, and me crossing my fingers that she did not take another job meanwhile.

[T-yolanda-11] **Interviewer:** What was happening during those eleven days?

[T-yolanda-12] **Yolanda:** Papers. Her contract, her bank details, the social security number, all of it going back and forth by email, and then the people in payroll, Nómina360, they have to create her in the system. She signed with me on the Tuesday. She could not clock in anywhere until that was done.

[T-yolanda-13] **Interviewer:** You mentioned you wrote to her first. Where do you keep names of people you might hire someday?

[T-yolanda-14] **Yolanda:** She laughs. "Tengo una lista en el WhatsApp personal. Los llamo mis 'maybes'." Ex-employees who left on good terms, friends of my crew, people who walked in like Laura. Right now there must be twenty-five names in there, more or less.

[T-yolanda-15] **Interviewer:** How does that list work in practice when a gap appears?

[T-yolanda-16] **Yolanda:** I open the chat, I send three or four messages, and by the evening I usually have someone. They already know the place, I already know them. Before anyone upstairs even opens a requisition, I have the person for Saturday.

[T-yolanda-17] **Interviewer:** Does any of that end up in the hiring system, HireDesk?

[T-yolanda-18] **Yolanda:** No. Not until HR forces it. "Cuando me lo piden, lo meto después, para que cuadren los números." She shrugs. The hire is real, the person is working, but in the system it appears late, when someone from the area notices and asks me to log it.

[T-yolanda-19] **Interviewer:** What has to be true before you can put someone new on the rota?

[T-yolanda-20] **Yolanda:** One thing above all: that they exist in Nómina360. Until payroll creates them, I cannot schedule them, full stop. "Y eso tarda de tres a seis días, siempre." I have had people standing at my door with the signed contract, ready, and the rota cannot carry them.

[T-yolanda-21] **Interviewer:** What happens during those three to six days, from your side?

[T-yolanda-22] **Yolanda:** Nothing good. I tell them to wait, I cover the shifts with overtime if I can, and I pray. Two summers ago I lost a girl like that after four days, a Burger King three streets down answered her faster. She was good. That one still bothers me.

[T-yolanda-23] **Interviewer:** When does hiring work get in the way of service? Tell me about the last time.

[T-yolanda-24] **Yolanda:** A Friday in July, peak of the season. I was in the back office scanning documents and copying data into forms while the dinner rush was building, and the drive-thru time went over five minutes. Me, the manager, hiding from my own window to do paperwork for someone who is not even hired yet.

[T-yolanda-25] **Interviewer:** Last question from my list: what would make you stop using a tool you were given?

[T-yolanda-26] **Yolanda:** If it costs me time or if I do not trust what it tells me. Look, "yo contrato gente que conozco. A un currículum no le debo nada." If a tool puts a stranger between me and the twenty-five names I already trust, I will use it once and never open it again.

[T-yolanda-27] **Interviewer:** We are at time. What did I not ask that I should have?

[T-yolanda-28] **Yolanda:** You asked about October. Ask me about July. In summer this restaurant goes from thirty-eight people to nearly fifty, and every one of them arrives through the same mess you just asked me about. Si sobrevivimos al verano, sobrevivimos a todo.

## Interviewer notes

- **What surprised me:** she hires from a personal WhatsApp "maybes" list (about 25 names) before any requisition exists, and those hires are logged in HireDesk late, only when HR asks, if at all [T-yolanda-14, T-yolanda-18].
- **What contradicts HQ's description:** HQ says candidates come from the ATS and job boards; her last hire came from a walk-in at the drive-thru window and she contacted her first, inverting the funnel [T-yolanda-08]. The real bottleneck is not screening: it is the 3 to 6 days between signing and existing in Nómina360, without which she cannot rota anyone [T-yolanda-20].
- **What I still do not know:** how many of her summer hires per year flow through the maybes list versus HireDesk, and what exactly payroll needs from her side to create someone in Nómina360 faster.
