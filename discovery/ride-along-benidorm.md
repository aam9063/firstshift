- **Hiring-related interruptions of operations:** 13 (rows flagged Yes during service blocks: 07:30, 08:15, 09:00, 09:20, 12:05, 12:30, 13:00, 16:40, 18:00, 19:30, 20:15, 22:20, 23:30).

- **Copy-paste events between systems:** 4 (HireDesk-to-phone numbers, Nómina360-to-ShiftGrid draft, documents re-keyed into Nómina360 form, rota cross-check against Nómina360).
- **Moments a candidate or new hire waits for a human:** 7 (14 HireDesk applicants, 1 voicemail, 1 maybe awaiting the 3-6 day system wait, Laura twice, 1 declined maybe whose gap reopens, 2 maybes messaged at close).
- **Key structural finding:** the manager's own informal pool (personal WhatsApp) is faster than every official channel; the official bottleneck is the hired-to-rota gap in Nómina360 [T-yolanda-20], and none of today's hiring work happened in HireDesk, which is where HQ believes the process lives.
