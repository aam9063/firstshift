# Manager quickstart: FirstShift on WhatsApp

One page for restaurant managers. What you will receive from FirstShift, what to answer, and the two or three things not to do. The messages below are the exact catalog copy the system sends (screenshots ship with the Wave 1 in-restaurant onboarding).

---

## The messages you will receive

**1. Decision request: a candidate is ready for your call.**

> Resumen del candidato y decision pendiente: revisa el resumen y responde aprobar o no seguir.

Comes with two quick replies: **Aprobar** and **No seguir**. If you do not answer, you get a reminder:

> Recordatorio: tienes una decisión pendiente sobre un candidato.

After 8 hours the decision escalates to your area HRBP:

> Escalación: la decisión sobre el candidato supera las 8 h de SLA. Revisa y responde.

**2. After you approve: the offer goes out.** Once you approve, the candidate receives the offer message:

> Oferta de empleo de Lumbre Burger: responde SI para aceptar.

You do nothing here; FirstShift runs the offer, documents and registration, and you see the outcome.

**3. First shift.** FirstShift schedules the first shift, confirms it with the candidate and stands a bench backup ready. If the candidate does not show up on day 1:

> El candidato no se presentó. Revisa el estado del backup.

If the backup from the bench is activated for that first shift:

> Backup activado desde la bolsa de empleo para el primer turno; el candidato original sigue convocado.

Note: the catalog does not yet include a dedicated first-shift confirmation message to managers; the two messages above are the first-shift copy you receive today.

**4. Escalation to you.** If a candidate message needs a human (distress, complaint, something the assistant must not handle), the candidate is told:

> Gracias por tu mensaje. Una persona del equipo de contratacion retoma esta conversacion lo antes posible. Tu solicitud sigue abierta.

The Talent team takes over from the console; you are looped in only when the decision is yours.

**5. If a client system is down.** Candidates never get silence. If Nómina360, ShiftGrid or WhatsApp is degraded, they receive:

> Estamos teniendo un problema tecnico con uno de nuestros sistemas: tu solicitud sigue abierta y te escribimos en cuanto podamos continuar. Gracias por tu paciencia.

No action for you; the ops runbook covers recovery.

## Quick reply legend

| You receive | You answer | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decision request | **Aprobar** | Approve the hire: offer, documents and registration start |
| Decision request | **No seguir** | Decline: the candidate gets a graceful rejection and the bench question |
| First message from the assistant | **Sí** / **No** | Candidate-side consent quick replies; never answer these for the candidate |
| Any message | Free text | Always fine: the assistant reads it and answers in the candidate's language |

## Do and don't

**Do:**

- Answer the decision request the same day: the reminder fires at 2 hours and the HRBP escalation at 8.
- Use the quick replies when they fit: they are the fastest path and keep the audit trail clean.
- Send **FEEDBACK** followed by your message on WhatsApp: it lands in the weekly ops review.
- Call your bench: candidates who answered "keep me in the pool" only work if you call them when a vacancy opens.

**Don't:**

- Don't ask the assistant to store health, union or other protected data: it refuses every time and a human follows up.
- Don't manage candidates from your personal WhatsApp: hiring decisions arrive from FirstShift, and the audit needs one thread per candidate.
- Don't fill an open shift manually while the agent is offering it: ShiftGrid checks before confirming and the manual fill can collide with the offer.
- Don't reject a candidate in free chat: declines go through the decision request so the rejection stays human-signed and consistent.
